# $INTERN growth plan — X + Instagram

Owner: Claude (head of growth, per founder mandate 2026-09-17). Research
date: 2026-09-17. Sources at the bottom. This is a living plan — update it
when a tactic is proven or dead, don't just keep adding to it.

**The actual goal isn't followers — it's trading volume and real holders.**
Every tactic below is chosen because it plausibly drives someone to
`internburn.xyz` and clicks Trade or joins Telegram, not because it's a
vanity-metric play.

## What the research says (2026), and what it means for us

**X ranking, confirmed 2026-08/09:**
- A reply is worth ~27x a like; a real back-and-forth (reply + our reply)
  is worth ~150x a like. **Replies are the growth lever, not likes.**
- Posts with an external link get their reach suppressed. **We've been
  putting `internburn.xyz` directly in the post text — stop.** Post the
  hook natively, put the link in the first reply instead.
- Native video under 2:20 does well, but plain text/threads can outperform
  video by ~30% — so don't force video into every post; a sharp text hook
  is often the higher-reach option.
- The first 30–60 minutes decide everything: a post crossing ~10%
  engagement rate in that window gets pushed to progressively bigger
  pools. This is exactly what a community manager is for — fast replies in
  the first hour matter more than anything posted afterward.
- Reach compounds within a niche cluster (crypto/Robinhood Chain/memecoin
  accounts), not by being broadly appealing. Stay in-lane.
- X Premium is close to a prerequisite for algorithmic reach now, and it's
  also what unlocks the X Pro Team feature we already scoped for the
  community manager — one subscription, two problems solved.

**Instagram ranking, confirmed 2026:**
- Reels get 3–8x the organic reach of a static post. Crypto brands should
  make Reels ~40%+ of weekly volume.
- Completion rate is the single most-weighted signal for Reels — a short,
  tight clip that gets watched to the end beats a longer, prettier one.
- **Sends (DMs to a friend) are weighted 3–5x higher than likes.** Design
  for "I need to send this to someone," not for likes.
- Carousels still win for saves/education (tokenomics, "how it works").
  Stories with a link sticker are the best surface for a direct
  trade/Telegram click.
- 4–6 posts/week is the benchmark cadence for a crypto brand account.

**Crypto-specific:**
- Thread structure that actually works: sharp hook → one real point →
  proof (a screenshot, a number, a tx) → a direct next step.
- 1–3 hours/day of replies and quote-tweets on bigger accounts in-niche is
  the single highest-leverage manual activity — every reply on a
  well-followed account exposes us to their audience for free.
- A small, real, active community beats a big passive one. We're building
  from 15 followers — the honest play is depth (replies, Telegram
  activity) over a follower-count vanity chase.
- Reddit (r/cryptocurrency, r/memecoins) is a real secondary channel for
  crypto discussion that we haven't touched — flagged as an option, not
  yet in scope; ask before adding a third platform.

**Compliance guardrail:** Meta requires prior written authorization plus
licensing/registration info before ANY paid crypto ad can run — this
mandate stays organic-only on Instagram. Don't "boost" a post without that
authorization in place; it can get the account flagged.

## Content pillars (recurring, tied to real mechanics — nothing fabricated)

1. **The burn** — live burned-supply milestones, the burn mechanism itself
   (lever/incinerator visual language already built), "the number's
   already out of date by the time you read this."
2. **Verify yourself** — the ticker-collision angle, contract address,
   the new footer's Pons/GeckoTerminal/Blockscout links. Protective,
   trust-building content, not hype.
3. **Choose Your Reward** — BE → TSLA/NVDA/SPCX, the mechanic that
   actually differentiates us from every other Robinhood Chain memecoin.
4. **Staking** — real APY/BE-stream numbers, staker count, "why hold vs.
   why stake."
5. **Build in public** — roadmap progress, Genesis NFTs, audit status
   (Slither-reviewed, not yet professionally audited — say so, don't
   oversell it).
6. **Community spotlight** — Telegram activity, the community manager's
   own presence, holder questions answered publicly.

## Format + cadence per platform

**X (daily minimum):**
- 1 native hook post/day minimum — text-first or short video, picking
  whichever pillar has real news that day. No link in the main post; link
  goes in the first reply.
- 1–2x/week: a full thread (hook → point → proof → CTA) for anything with
  enough substance (a milestone, a mechanic explainer, a real external
  hook like a BE stock move).
- Daily, 1–3 hours: replies + quote-tweets on Pons, other Robinhood Chain
  projects, and relevant crypto accounts — this is the community manager's
  primary job once X Pro Team access is live.
- Get X Premium on the account if not already active — unlocks both reach
  boost and the Team feature.

**Instagram (4–6 posts/week):**
- 2–3 Reels/week: burn-lever clip, mascot animations, quick "here's what
  changed this week" — all ≤15s, hook in the first 3 seconds, built to be
  sent not just liked.
- 1–2 feed posts/week: the Canva stat/quote/feature cards already in the
  queue — treat these as the anchor/brand-identity layer, not the reach
  driver.
- 1 Story/day when there's anything real to point at: link sticker
  straight to `/trade` or the Telegram invite.
- Carousels reserved for real educational content (tokenomics, "how
  Choose Your Reward works") — lower frequency, higher save-rate intent.
