## The rules
1. Reply within 15 to 30 minutes of a target post. Early replies get seen.
2. Add one useful thing: a number, a correction, a link-free insight, or a
   sharp question. No "gm", no "great post".
3. No links, no @-mentions of companies we're not replying to, no asking for
   follows. Our profile does the selling.
4. Mention $INTERN only when it's genuinely relevant. About 1 reply in 4.
5. Never reply to scam or "DM me" accounts. Block and report.
6. Log the best 3 replies of the day so we can see what works.
