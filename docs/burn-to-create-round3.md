# Burn to Create, Round 3 — rules and break-even math

Live 2026-09-26 16:00 UTC to 2026-10-03 12:00 UTC (about 7 days). Replaces the
Round 2 5x/4x/3x match for new entries. Entries already submitted under Round 2
are still judged under Round 2's terms.

## Rules

1. Burn at least $5 of $INTERN during the round. "Buyback and burn": buy it, then
   burn it via Video Credits on the site or by sending it to the dead address
   `0x000000000000000000000000000000000000dEaD`. Keep the transaction hash.
2. Make any video that features our mascots/interns (Blaze, Rendo, Promptly,
   Synapse, Hush or Cache). Any tool works; the intern has to be in the video.
3. Post it on X, tag @InternburnHQ, and include your wallet and burn tx hash.
   Submit the same link on internburn.xyz/burn-to-create.
4. The team picks the top 5 by hand: creativity, how well the interns are used,
   and reach. One prize per person; wallets that share token flows count as one.
5. Paid in a tokenized stock of the winner's choice (TSLA, NVDA, SPCX or BE),
   valued in USD at the close of the round, within 7 days of results.

## Break-even math

- Buying $INTERN on the curve pays the creator wallet about 1.72% of the
  purchase in fees (measured on-chain, see competition-2-rules.md).
- **Prize pool = 0.5% of all $ burned by entrants during the round.** Burn
  purchases also count toward Competition #2's pool (1% of volume), so the two
  pools together take 1.5% out of the 1.72% those purchases earn. That keeps
  both rounds break-even with a small margin left for the 70/20/10 split.
- Places unlock with the pool, as in Competition #2: under $25 pays 1st only,
  $25 to $99 pays the top 3 (50/30/20), $100 or more pays the top 5
  (40/25/15/10/10).

| $ burned by entrants | Fee income (1.72%) | Pool (0.5%) | Places | Prizes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $500 | $8.60 | $2.50 | 1st only | $2.50 |
| $2,000 | $34.40 | $10 | 1st only | $10 |
| $5,000 | $86 | $25 | top 3 | $12.50 / $7.50 / $5 |
| $10,000 | $172 | $50 | top 3 | $25 / $15 / $10 |
| $25,000 | $430 | $125 | top 5 | $50 / $31.25 / $18.75 / $12.50 / $12.50 |
| $50,000 | $860 | $250 | top 5 | $100 / $62.50 / $37.50 / $25 / $25 |

Break-even assumption: the burned tokens were bought during the round (the
usual "buyback and burn"). Burning an older holding adds no fee income, but the
pool is only 0.5% of the burn, so the worst case is small. Every burn also
shrinks supply for all holders, which is the real prize for the ecosystem.

At today's volumes the cash pool will be small. The pool grows with the burn,
and winners also get featured on X. A fixed team seed (for example $50) makes
day one bigger but is not break-even below about $10,000 burned.
