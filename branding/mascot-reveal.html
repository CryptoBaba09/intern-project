Why an original mascot

01

Meme characters are still owned

Pepe is Matt Furie's copyright, the Simpsons is Fox's, Pudgy Penguins is an active registered brand with its own legal team. Crypto's casual reuse of them is tolerated, not licensed — a real risk the moment a project gets attention.

02

Owned IP compounds

Blaze can front the marketplace, the socials, merch, and every future intern in the roster without asking anyone's permission — and the equity it builds stays $INTERN's, not on loan from someone else's character.

03

Built for the sticker test

Flat color, thick outline, one readable expression at 40px — the same shape logic that makes the best crypto mascots survive being screenshotted, cropped, and pasted into a hundred reply threads.

04

Already on-brand

Same green (#00C805) and ember (#D9A441) already running through the site and the burn mechanic — Blaze isn't a new identity bolted on, it's the existing one given a face.
